How to Stop Settling for Potential
English transcript
Situations where we kind of catch ourselves thinking things like “but I know who they could be, I
know their potential, if they just changed XYZ then everything would be amazing.” And basically we
keep ourselves in that situation where we are not really fulfilled, and we struggle to let go and to
actually look at the reality of the person that they are right now.
So what does it mean when we're settling for potential? One sign might be when we find ourselves
constantly thinking about the future, not the present moment — “if it was like this…” If you catch
yourself fantasising about moments with them where they're actually very different from how they
are now, that's a sign of our own making that we are not actually looking at the person that they are
right now.
So you might be feeling like you are waiting: waiting to finally connect, to get commitment, waiting
for them to get their life together, waiting, sitting, hoping one day it's going to be different. That's a
clear sign that we're more attached to the potential of them than to the actual person they are.
You also will notice that you just continue to complain to your friends about the same thing again
and again and again, and you are struggling to let it go.
And that's something you will see in the dynamics — that you probably forgive the same behaviour
again and again. You will have the same conversation without any changes. It takes time in a
relationship to shift a dynamic; it's not going to be with one conversation, but you will see changes,
little ones. If there is no change happening, we are holding on to potential.

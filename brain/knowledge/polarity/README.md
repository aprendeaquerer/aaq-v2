# Polarity Domain

Use this domain for masculine/feminine polarity, grounded presence, receptivity, directionality, desire, embodied leadership, and consent-aware relational dynamics.

Avoid rigid gender stereotypes, manipulation tactics, or advice that removes agency from either partner.

## Lanes

Use `polarity_lane` to separate audience-specific polarity material:

- `masculine_advice`: written for men or masculine-led partners practicing grounded direction, presence, pursuit, protection, truth, repair, and desire without control.
- `feminine_advice`: reserved for future feminine-led material.
- `shared_principle`: useful to any polarity expression.

Audience-specific articles may speak directly to men or women, but should not present gender traits as universal facts. The framing should stay choice-based, consent-aware, and relationally safe.

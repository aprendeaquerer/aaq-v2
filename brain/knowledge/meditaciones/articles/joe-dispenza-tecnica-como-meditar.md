# TÉCNICA: cómo meditar correctamente (la mayoría lo hace mal) — Joe Dispenza

## Transcripción

Let's be honest, most people think they know how to meditate, but they're doing it completely
wrong. They sit down, close their eyes, and then try to force their mind to go silent. And within
seconds, their thoughts get louder, more chaotic, more uncontrollable. So, they assume, "I
can't meditate." But that's not true. You're not failing at meditation.
You were just taught the wrong way, because meditation is not about stopping your thoughts.
It's about changing your relationship with them. And once you understand this, everything
changes. Here's the first thing you need to know. Your mind is designed to think.
That's its job. Trying to stop your thoughts is like trying to stop your heart from beating. The
more you try to control it, the more resistance you create. And that's why beginners struggle.
They think meditation equals silence, but real meditation is awareness.
It's the ability to observe your thoughts without getting pulled into them. Let me explain it
simply. Imagine your thoughts are like clouds in the sky. They're constantly moving,
sometimes fast, sometimes slow, sometimes heavy, but the sky never changes. You are not
the clouds, you are the sky, and meditation is the practice of remembering that.
Now, here's where most people go wrong. The moment a thought appears, they engage with
it. They analyze it. They judge it. They follow it.
And suddenly, they're lost in a story. 5 minutes pass, and they don't even realize it. That's not
meditation. That's unconscious thinking. Meditation begins the moment you notice that you got
lost.
That moment of awareness is the win, not silence, not perfection. Awareness. So, how do you
actually meditate properly? It starts with something very simple. Your breath.
Not because breathing is special, but because it's always happening. It gives your attention
something stable to rest on. You don't need to control your breath. You don't need to change
it. You just notice it.
The feeling of air going in and out. That's it. Now, your mind will wander. It will think about your
day, your problems, your future. And this is where real meditation happens because instead of
getting frustrated, you simply notice, "Oh, I'm thinking." And then you bring your attention back,
gently.
No force, no judgment. This process right here is meditation. Not staying focused forever, but
returning again and again. Think about it like training a muscle. Every time your mind wanders
and you bring it back, you're building awareness.
You're strengthening control over your attention. And over time, this changes everything
because most people don't control their mind, their mind controls them. Meditation flips that.
Now, let's talk about something important. You don't need the perfect environment.

You don't need music. You don't need candles. You don't need a specific position. You can
meditate anywhere, sitting, lying down, even walking because meditation is not about where
you are, it's about how aware you are. Another mistake people make is expecting instant
results.
They try it once or twice and say it didn't work, but meditation is not a quick fix. It's a skill and
like any skill, it takes consistency. But here's the truth, the benefits start immediately, even if it
doesn't feel like it because every time you sit, you are training your mind to slow down, to
observe, to detach and that builds something powerful. Clarity. Over time, you'll start noticing
changes.
You react less. You think more clearly. You feel calmer even in stressful situations and that's
the real purpose of meditation, not just feeling peaceful while sitting, but carrying that peace
into your life. Now, let me give you the simplest way to start. Sit comfortably, close your eyes,
focus on your breath.
When your mind wanders, bring it back. That's it. No complications, no overthinking, just
practice this. And remember this, you are not trying to become someone new. You are simply
returning to who you already are beneath the noise because peace is not something you
create, it's something you uncover.
So if you've ever felt like you can't meditate, now you know the truth. You can. You just
needed the right understanding.

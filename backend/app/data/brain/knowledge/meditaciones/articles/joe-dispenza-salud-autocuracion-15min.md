settle into this moment. This is your time. A time to let go, to tune in, and to elevate your
energy.

breath.

here to heal. It is here to restore.
It is here to awaken the dormant energy that has always been inside of you. Now bring your
awareness to the space between your eyebrows, your third eye. This is the gateway to higher
consciousness. Imagine it glowing with a powerful luminous energy. Let this energy expand.
Feel it activate the very core of your being. You are worthy of healing. You are worthy of
transformation. You are worthy of the life you [Music] desire. Breathe deeply.
Hold this breath at the top. Let the energy rise through your spine, passing through each

Hold this energy. Let it surge through you like an electric current of possibility. And now [Music]

Feel yourself stepping into a new reality. You are healing. You are transforming. You are
stepping into the highest version of yourself. Allow yourself to settle into this moment.
Bring your awareness to the energy that is already moving within you. You may not see it, but
